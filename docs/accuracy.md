# Prediction accuracy

Generated by `npm run accuracy` from 6 recorded race session(s) (5 capture file(s)). Every
prediction is scored against what then happened in the same race, next to a naive baseline - a model
only earns its place on the dashboard if it beats the obvious guess.

## "If you box now" - rejoin position

Just before any car (all 22, not only the player) stopped, the rejoin model predicted where it would come
out; that is compared with its position 25 s after the stop. Green-flag stops only.

| | Exact | Within 1 place | Mean error |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Model** (pit loss measured live from the field) | **33%** | **67%** | **1.33 places** |
| Same model, fixed per-track pit loss | 33% | 67% | 1.33 places |
| Naive: "you keep your position" | 0% | 0% | 4.33 places |

3 stops scored - a small sample: the races recorded so far had few green-flag stops (most stops in them came under a red flag, which is excluded), so read these as a sanity check rather than a benchmark.

## Projected battery at the line

At half-distance on a green lap, the projected ERS store at the line (the rest of the lap assumed to go
like the same stretch of the last lap) against the store when the car crossed it. Every car on track.

| | Mean error | Within 5 points |
|---|---|---|
| **Projection** | **6.8 pts** | **58%** |
| Naive: "stays where it is now" | 12.2 pts | 26% |

471 half-laps scored. Points = percentage points of a full 4 MJ store.

## Tyre wear model

At the end of each race, every car's green-flag laps are predicted from a wear fit on the *other*
cars (held out, so a car never grades its own fit), up to that car's own pace level - only the slope
is tested. Against the naive "tyres don't wear" guess. Error per lap, slow laps (spins, traffic over
7%) left out.

| Race | Laps (cars) | **Model** median / mean | Naive median / mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silverstone | 71 (22) | **200 / 338 ms** | 180 / 334 ms |
| Suzuka | 70 (22) | **116 / 342 ms** | 189 / 366 ms |
| Monaco | 134 (22) | **133 / 205 ms** | 422 / 440 ms |
| Montreal | 63 (21) | **92 / 192 ms** | 169 / 255 ms |
| Barcelona | 154 (21) | **319 / 388 ms** | 366 / 491 ms |

It beats the naive guess on 4 of 5 races; not on Silverstone (71 laps).

## Attack forecast ("where will the car behind attack?")

Of 33 real passes on the player in 4 race(s) (from each capture's `npm run analyze` report), 6 came at the
corner the forecast named, against 7 at the track's single busiest passing corner and 4.5 expected
from a random corner. **It beats chance, but not yet the simpler "busiest corner" rule**, so the dashboard
words it as a "likely spot" and it stays marked experimental until more races are recorded.
