Features

Only what the game can't tell you

Pit timing

Every lap you could box on, raced to the flag. Where you'd rejoin, where you'd finish.

Battle calls

Attack or defend, a trained pass-chance model, and each car's battery at the line.

Ask the engineer

"Engineer, gap behind?" "Engineer, when do I box?" Ask out loud, hands-free, and hear the answer.

Weather in laps

Rain in ~5 laps, not minutes, and whether inters or slicks are faster right now.

Debrief

Result, pace, lap-by-lap telemetry, and where you lose time to your rivals.

Any screen

Tablet, phone, laptop or a second monitor. Scan a QR code and it's on the same Wi-Fi.
